## Question

ADR-0006 states that year-end migration is destructive and that "only the backup preserves" the deleted data — a claim that ADR-0012 then records as unmet, because the Drive path does not function. This map makes an archive that genuinely does preserve it, for ESL. What does the existing ADR record set say about that, and what does this map owe it?

## Sub-questions

- **Relationship to ADR-0006.** It is scoped to CAS and is not amended by ESL work. But its _reasoning_ — "only the backup preserves them" — is the belief this map is repairing. Does ESL archiving need a note on ADR-0006, or is leaving it untouched correct because the scopes are disjoint (as ADR-0022 concluded)?
- **ADR-0012.** It lists the Drive cold archive as in-scope-but-unbuilt. Does this map discharge that consequence, partially, for ESL? Its Tier-B story is exactly "the perpetual year-end snapshot for the accreditation obligation" — is the ESL archive that thing, for one department?
- **ADR-0022.** It states ESL year advancement "operates on disjoint tables" from the CAS migration and "they are not unified". Archiving does not unify them, but it does add a second deletion path next to `advanceGrade`. Confirm no amendment is owed.
- **The new ADR's number and scope.** Does archiving a year warrant one ADR, or two (the archive mechanism, and the read-only browser)?
- **The glossary.** `CONTEXT.md` defines "Year-end migration snapshot" as "the backbone of the 5-year records archive". Is the ESL archive a second kind of that, needing its own term?

## Why this matters

Without this, the repo ends up with an implemented archive that three ADRs still describe as non-existent or non-functioning.

Blocked by #146 (file shape) and #143 (deletion set) — the ADR's decision section is written from them.
