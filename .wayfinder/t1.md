## Question

The ESL department stops at grade 10 — grades 11 and 12 do not offer ESL classes. `advanceGrade` already carries grades 7 and 8 into the next year and writes only into `toYear`, so prior years are never touched. What actually _leaves_ Convex when a year is archived?

## Why this is the first decision

It sets the meaning of "archive" for everything downstream — what the file contains, what the browser shows, and whether the operation is reversible. Guessing wrong here means the file and the deletion disagree.

## Sub-questions

- **The grade-10 leavers.** A grade-10 ESL student in `2025-2026` does not continue to ESL. When that year is archived, do their `esl_students` rows go with it, or do they persist as history the way they do today?
- **The cohorts and classes.** Every `esl_cohorts` row for the year, and every `esl_classes` row pointing at one? Note `purgeOrphanedClasses` already deletes classes whose cohort is gone, so ordering matters.
- **Every year, or only closed ones?** Is an archive ever written for a year that is not the immediately-prior one?
- **Reversibility.** After the deletion, is the year's state recoverable from the file alone? What does the file need to contain for that to be true?

## Constraints

- Archiving is **read-only history** (settled in grilling). Restoring a year over live data is out of scope.
- The deletion must not break live reads: `list`, `getById`, and every admin page currently read cohorts without a year filter.
- ADR-0022 established that a student has one `esl_students` row per cohort per year — so a student appearing in two archived years is normal, not a duplicate.

## Out of scope

- CAS/HWIS students and evaluations.
- The daily backup snapshot's contents.
